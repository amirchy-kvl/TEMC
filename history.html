Since 2001

Two decades of steady progress

Founded in 2001, The Eureka Marketing Co. has spent more than two decades supporting the modernization of Bangladesh's aquaculture sector, under the continued leadership of Mostafa Asadullah Chowdhury.

2001
Company Founded
2005
Began promoting awareness of the significance of the Halda River
2008
Expanded business operations across all 64 districts of Bangladesh
2012
Awarded the Third Best Private Entrepreneur Stall at the Central Fish Fair during National Fish Week 2012
2016
Specialized business operations nationwide
2020–2023
Initiated multiple field trips under national projects focused on the Halda River
A continuing story

Experience that keeps moving forward

Our history is rooted in practical service: bringing useful technology closer to hatcheries, sharing field knowledge, and keeping Bangladesh's aquaculture heritage at the center of our work.
