Supporting Bangladesh's Aquaculture Industry Since 2001

For more than two decades, The Eureka Marketing Co. has supported Bangladesh's aquaculture sector through the import, distribution, and application of modern fisheries technology, hatchery equipment, water quality solutions, medications, and technical expertise.

Along with rigorous endeavours in the modern aquaculture domain, pioneering awareness activities, and field-level work along the Halda River — South Asia's largest natural tidal carp breeding center — our work reflects a long-standing commitment to advancing sustainable fisheries across Bangladesh.

Who we are

About The Eureka Marketing Co.

We are a Bangladesh-based aquaculture company specializing in fisheries technology, hatchery equipment, industrial aeration systems, APIs, medications, water quality solutions, and technical consultation.

Since 2001, we have worked alongside fish farmers, hatcheries, educational institutions, and fisheries professionals, contributing practical experience developed through decades of involvement in Bangladesh's aquaculture sector. Our Founder and CEO, Mostafa Asadullah Chowdhury, has collaborated with multiple aquaculture firms, universities, and exhibitors around the world.
